Feed & clothe the community

Canned goods and produce through our Food Bank partnership, plus a thrift store offering affordable, gently used clothes and household items.

The thrift store

The thrift store is currently under renovation and open with limited hours. It carries gently used and new in-season merchandise for men, women, boys, girls, children, and infants — all at affordable prices for the neighborhood.

Donate items

Item donations are by appointment only. We accept gently used or new clothing and household goods, and we're happy to help unload heavy or bulky items. Call (423) 206-2866 to schedule a drop-off.
