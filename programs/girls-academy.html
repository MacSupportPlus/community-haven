Girls Academy

A teen academy that educates, empowers, and advocates for girls affected by trauma.

"So You Think You Can Date?"

Girls Academy runs a year-round mentoring program for teen girls, and hosts an annual "Bravery Brunch" honoring local domestic-violence survivors. Since 2018, the program has helped support the escape of more than 19 women from abusive situations and delivered programming to over 60 teen girls and college-age students in and around Tennessee.

"Surviving is important, but living for real after is the key."

Founded and led by Keosha Brewster-Holloway, Executive Director of The Heard.

Program founder

Keosha Brewster-Holloway

Founder, The Heard

Born and raised in Chattanooga, TN. A survivor of childhood sexual violence that led to years of domestic violence, Keosha founded The Heard, a nonprofit that educates, empowers, and advocates for marginalized women and girls affected by trauma.

Related support: G.R.I.E.V.E. for families affected by violent loss, and Anger & Trauma Management.
