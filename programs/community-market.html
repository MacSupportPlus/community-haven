Community Market

A weekly Saturday market on the block, open to food and merchandise vendors from the neighborhood and beyond.

When it's open

Every Saturday, 11:00am–6:00pm, from the first Saturday in April through the last Saturday in October. Check our events calendar for cancellations or schedule changes.

Become a vendor

Vendor space is limited and offered first-come, first-served. Vendors must register and prepay at least one month prior to their Saturday. The vendor fee is $20 per Saturday and is non-refundable.

Questions before you apply? Email thecommunityhaven@gmail.com.
