Youth Development Initiative

An eight-topic work-readiness series delivered over four weeks, building real skills for teens headed into the workforce.

How the program runs

Facilitators lead two 60-minute topics per week, twice a week, over four weeks — eight topics in total. Cohorts run about ten participants at a time, with self-paced virtual assignments between sessions.

The eight topics

  • Employment Basics
  • The Job Search
  • Dress to Impress
  • The Interview
  • Job Retention
  • Work Ethics
  • Money Management
  • Entrepreneurship
