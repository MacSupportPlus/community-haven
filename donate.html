Every gift stays on this block, for this block.

The Community Haven is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your support funds Community Patrol, the food bank and thrift store, youth development, and the Girls Academy & G.R.I.E.V.E. — all working the same 5-acre block in Bushtown.

Give by mail

Right now, the most direct way to give is by check or money order, made payable to The Community Haven:

815 N Hickory Street
Chattanooga, TN 37404

We're setting up online giving next — if you'd like to give today by another method (card over the phone, a matching-gift program through your employer, or a recurring gift), reach out and we'll make it work.

Email us about giving (423) 206-2866

Other ways to help

  • Donate gently used clothing or household items to the thrift store
  • Volunteer with Community Patrol or a youth program
  • Ask your employer about matching gifts
  • Follow and share what we're doing on Facebook
